# FairRoom: 100-second demo recording

Do not record keys, private Messages threads, or account dashboards containing credentials. Record only the consenting test conversation and sample group. No polished video has been exported yet; this is the shot list and narration for the real recording.

## Before recording
Keep the Mac awake, Messages signed in, FairRoom on port 4300 and ngrok running. Confirm three sample profiles are ready. Do not press Reset demo or start the reset-based tutorial immediately before the phone sequence. Meet FairRoom now preserves the existing sample. Rehearse the commands below once. The Mac bridge responds from Ashfaq's own identity, so the brother sends commands. This is a two-person live demo of a three-profile housing search, not a verified three-party bot group.

## 0–12 seconds: problem
Show landing page, then the consenting Messages conversation.
Narration: “Finding a shared home means reconciling three budgets, three wish lists, and rooms that aren't equally valuable. FairRoom turns that conversation into a shortlist the whole group can consider.”

## 12–35 seconds: search
Brother sends `FairRoom find homes`. Show the actual arriving recommendations, then open C1's link.
Narration: “These are labeled sample homes. The same matching engine checks each person's requirements and proposes individual room prices. It won't silently drop a must-have just to produce a result.”

## 35–53 seconds: room prices
Brother sends `FairRoom split C1`. Show $1,520 / $1,280 / $1,040, then the website's Review split drawer if useful.
Narration: “A private bathroom or bigger room can cost more. Shared costs are included, every cent reconciles, and the split is a proposal—not an automatic charge. Changing it invalidates previous approvals.”

## 53–70 seconds: trust
On Homes expand Before you commit; show estimated utilities and missing move-in fees. Or brother sends `FairRoom check C1`.
Narration: “A cheap listing isn't a complete price. FairRoom separates recorded facts from unknowns and prepares the questions to ask before committing.”

## 70–85 seconds: stay in chat
Brother sends `FairRoom like C1`, then `FairRoom saved`.
Narration: “Save the home and retrieve it right in the conversation. The website is there when you want detail.”

## 85–100 seconds: sponsor evidence (only after real runs)
Show Factory's completed review and a concise Chalk evaluation result, without keys.
Narration AFTER successful runs: “Factory's Droid SDK reviews saved listing evidence through bounded tools. Chalk routes natural-language intent, and we test its responses against a repeatable evaluation set. Deterministic code handles the actual housing constraints and money.”
If credentials are not connected, omit this segment and do not list those integrations as completed.
